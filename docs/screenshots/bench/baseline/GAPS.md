# Real-look bench: baseline gaps

Judged on 3 Nov 2026 by a fresh-context critic who was given the images and references and no opinion of them (see [`eval/look/README.md`](../../../../eval/look/README.md)). It's one judge's opinion, not a measurement, and the home is one fixed sample.

**References:** `living.jpg`, `bedroom.jpg` and `hero.jpg` are from floorplan-3d-skill; `still.jpg` is from Casa Serra. They were used privately for comparison and aren't in this repo. All four are foreign marketing images and none is a daytime interior, so shots 01 to 05 have no direct daylight benchmark, and "better" means more believable as a render, not truer to an Indian home. **No photos of real Indian homes were available yet.**

## Shot by shot

- **01-eye-living:** Passes as neither, but reads as a charming cosy-game still rather than a test screenshot. First giveaway: the puffy mustard armchair and blob cat, on contour-map marble under a flat brown ceiling. `living.jpg` is clearly better. Live is close to the photo, with jagged fan blades and door panels, and its crop loses most of the doorway.
- **02-eye-bedroom:** Fails both. First giveaway: a morning window barely brighter than the walls and no sun in the room, then a faceted lampshade at lower right and a bed that is a teal slab. `bedroom.jpg` is far better. Live adds a dirty smudge beside the wardrobe and crops the window.
- **03-eye-kitchen:** Fails both; reads as an empty game corridor. First giveaway: the confetti floor, then basement-dull light at noon. No kitchen reference; `living.jpg` is the closest interior and is far better. Live is softer, with jagged edges on the hood.
- **04-eye-bathroom:** Fails both. A toilet and a thin window show, but through a slot: an oversized door and dark shower glass fill about 70% of the frame, and no basin is visible. No bathroom reference; `bedroom.jpg` is the closest and is far better. Live adds blotchy walls and a jagged handle.
- **05-eye-window:** Fails both. First giveaway: fog and the top of a boundary wall outside, no brighter than the room. `living.jpg` is better: its skyline is garish and doesn't belong to the room, but it has depth. Live is the same, with a dirty smudge around the sofa back.
- **06-street-front:** Passes as neither; it is a good stylised game still. First giveaway: toy picket gate, faceted water tank, empty gradient sky with no neighbours. `still.jpg`, the only daylight outdoor image, is more polished, but ours has the more believable sun on a wall.
- **07-street-gate:** Passes as neither, but it is the best in the set and closest to a stylised pro render at thumbnail size. First giveaway: blob plants and printed-looking grass, then cut-out clothes. `still.jpg` is more polished, ours is the stronger arrival picture.
- **08-aerial:** Not a photo of a model, but passes as a clean stylised illustration of one. First giveaway: a big empty brown slab dominates, with small dark rooms and nothing glowing. `hero.jpg` is far better; ours is only clearer about the street and the plot.
- **09-night-living:** Passes as neither, but is warm at a glance and the closest of the interiors. First giveaway: the far window glows bright cream with a halo, as if it were daytime, while the open door beside it shows navy night. `living.jpg` and `hero.jpg` are better. Live has the same faults, plus door panel lines breaking into dashes and a door jamb that changes colour abruptly.
- **10-reel-room:** Fails both; reads as a mobile game. First giveaway: the rest of the home under milky fog, a scooter and steps turned to white plaster, and road dashes on the grass. `hero.jpg` is far better, though this living room reads quickly.

## The ten biggest gaps, biggest first

1. **Rooms aren't lit by daylight.** Almost no light from the windows reaches the room. The bedroom, bathroom and kitchen show no sun patch at all, and 01 has only small warm touches. Morning and noon look like dusk, with olive-grey walls and a flat khaki lid of a ceiling. *Shots 01–05. Impact: high.* Without Indian photos the critic can't say how bright these rooms should be, but this dim at noon reads wrong regardless.
2. **Everything looks like a toy.** Puffy armchair and sofa, blob cat, faceted lampshade rim, side-table base and toilet lid, plants made of green balls, door panels that look painted on, cut-out clothes. *All shots, worst in 01, 02, 04, 07. Impact: high.*
3. **The floors give it away.** The marble looks like a contour map (01, 09) and like purple-grey doodled carpet from above (10). The confetti terrazzo is the busiest thing in 03, and the wood is patchwork in 02. The living-room marble catches some light, but as a lone glowing spot with no visible source. *Shots 01, 02, 03, 09, 10. Impact: high.* Chip size and veining can't be checked against real Indian floors yet, though the contour-map look is wrong either way.
4. **There is no world outside.** Every window shows the same fog and the same wall-top pier. The sunset in 01's doorway is pretty, but nothing is out there. The front elevation in 06 stands against an empty gradient, and its windows show flat shapes with no sky reflected. *Shots 01, 02, 04, 05, 06. Impact: medium.*
5. **Some pictures are still badly taken.** 01 and 09 have real depth. 04 is mostly door and glass; 02 has a lampshade in the lens, a chopped bed and a wardrobe blocking the window; 03 is a tunnel; 05 has a sofa-back lump at the bottom. Walls lean in 01, 02 and 04, and the living room and kitchen read as corridors. *Shots 02–05, traces in 01 and 09. Impact: medium.* Part of this is the sample home's long, narrow rooms, and part is the bench's fixed camera.
6. **Most rooms look undressed.** The living room is fine, with framed photos, a sideboard, tea and a newspaper. But no window in any shot has a curtain. The bedroom has a huge blank wall and a bed with no layering, the kitchen a long bare wall and a stranded fridge, and the bathroom shows only fixtures. *Shots 02–05; curtains also 01, 09. Impact: medium.*
7. **Night still has faults that look like errors.** The warm mood is good and the table lamp's shade reads as lit. But the window glows as if in daylight beside a navy doorway, a strip of wall by the door turns saturated navy, and the far ceiling drops to dark. The floor lamp's shade stays opaque while the wall beside it burns white. *Shot 09. Impact: medium.*
8. **The walking view is uneven and jagged.** The live versions of 01 and 09 hold up well against their photos, while 02, 04 and 05 look dirty, with smudges and blotchy walls. All show stair-stepped edges, and the tall crop loses the doorway in 01 and much of the window in 02. *All `-live` images. Impact: medium.*
9. **The bird's-eye views don't sell the rooms.** Shot 08 is mostly an empty brown slab with dim rooms. Shot 10 buries the home in milky fog, with white objects and road dashes on grass. `hero.jpg` sells every room at a glance. *Shots 08, 10. Impact: medium.*
10. **Outdoors is spotless and the ground looks printed.** The plaster has no stains or wear, the grass is a blotchy mat, and the picket gate looks like a Western cottage's. *Shots 06, 07, 08, 10. Impact: low.*

## Already good, must not be lost

- **Sun on the outside walls (06, 07).** Long railing and balcony shadows on warm peach plaster are the most believable thing in the set. No reference shows sun on a wall better.
- **Lived-in Indian detail.** Toran, chappals at the door, tulsi stand, washing on the balcony, overhead tank, ceiling fan, pressure cooker, floor cushions, family photos, tea and newspaper, and a sleeping cat. The toran against the sunset in 01's open doorway is the best moment indoors. The references look like generic show flats by comparison.
- **The model on its plinth (08).** The home, plot and street read at once on a phone-shaped image, with a calm backdrop and soft ground shadow.
